Waypoint bookmarks

Show your published tours on any website, in your own browser only, without installing anything on that website. It's for building and demoing tours before the real snippet is added.

Find it on your project's Install page in Waypoint.

🧭 Waypoint preview 🛠 Waypoint builder

Enter a valid key to activate the button.

Check what your site knows about the logged-in user

Waypoint can recognise logged-in users without changing the site's code, if the pages already hold a user id. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar, log in to the website, then click it. It lists the ids it finds. Email addresses and phone numbers are hidden, and nothing is sent anywhere.

🔎 Waypoint login check

Then, in Waypoint, open the project and set Recognise logged-in users to what it suggests.

Set it up once

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: in Chrome on a Mac press ⌘ Shift B (Windows: Ctrl Shift B).
  2. Drag both buttons, Waypoint preview and Waypoint builder, onto your bookmarks bar.
  3. In Waypoint, add the website's address (for example https://www.example.com) to the project's Allowed origins.

Build a tour by clicking

  1. In Waypoint, open your tour and click Open visual builder. Copy the builder code.
  2. Open the website, then click Waypoint builder in your bookmarks bar.
  3. Paste the code and click Connect. Then click + Add step and click any element on the page.
  4. If the page fully reloads, click Waypoint builder again: you stay signed in for two hours.

Show a tour

  1. Open the website and go to the page where your tour starts.
  2. Click Waypoint preview in your bookmarks bar. Your published tour appears.
  3. Clicking it again replays the tour from the start.
  4. If the page fully reloads during a tour, click the button again to continue where you were.

Good to know

Only you see the tour, and only after clicking the button. Nothing is installed on the website. Real visitors see tours only after the site's team adds the install snippet (for example through Google Tag Manager).
